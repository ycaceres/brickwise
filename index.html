Session chart

How to read the chart

  • Actual pace, on the left scale (min/km).
  • Actual HR, on the right scale (bpm).
  • Actual incline, in the lower part (no scale; the value is in the tooltip).
  • Dashed lines: the plan. Red is the step's HR cap or goal; green is the planned incline.
  • Background blocks: the whole workout. Width = duration, height = speed, color = zone. What is done gets dimmed and the white bar marks where you are.
  • Bottom ribbon (α1): with a chest strap, heart rate variability (DFA α1 over the last 2 min). Green: aerobic (> 0.75); orange: transition (0.5–0.75); red: above the anaerobic threshold (< 0.5); gray: computing or noisy signal.
  • Side columns: pace zones (left) and HR zones (right), aligned at the same height. On top, the zone you are in right now.
